# Catalogue review sheet

Items to confirm with Tenacity Locks before the catalogue is shared widely. Everything else was read directly from the product photos (`data/products.json`, field `source_path`).

## Assets
- 118 unique product photos: 14 loose images in `images/`, 104 from the ZIPs (extracted to `images/extracted/`). Every one is used exactly once.
- The 17 ZIP archives contain duplicates of each other (e.g. `10.43.39.zip` = `10.53.12.zip`); duplicates were removed by checking file contents.
- Not used: 2 videos (`WhatsApp Video 2026-10-06 at 09.30.02.mp4`, Big F6 group; `09.46.49.mp4`, deadlock group).
- No Tenacity logo file was supplied, so the catalogue uses a plain text wordmark.

## Open questions
1. **TB68 MCF / TB68 SNCP**: the brief lists them as Category 2 examples, but their photos sit with TB68 MAC in the Category 1 group (same poster: single pin, 3 normal keys, KSh 4,000). They are shown under **Category 1**.
2. **Premium internal locks (section 02)**: the brief mentions a "T78 GP". The photo reads **T189 GP**, which is the label used.
3. **Rose-handle single-pin and 3-pin locks** (S01–S04, KSh 4,000 / 5,000) are in section 02, Premium Internal. They arrived in the same photo group as the T189. Confirm they are sold as internal locks.
4. **Cisa Night Latch** (KSh 4,500) arrived in the auto-latching deadlock group and is shown there, but it is a rim night latch, not a mortice deadlock.
5. **Viro 303 PV**: the headline says 303PV and the shelf label says "303 PV", but the small caption reads "50 mm | 313".
6. **Viro 102**: the photo shows only the model and price (KSh 4,500), no size or key details.
7. **Bathroom sets**: the contents listed (towel rail, ring, tumbler holder, soap dish, hook, toilet-roll holder) are read from the photos. Confirm what is in the box.
8. **Finish codes** (MAB, MAC, MCF, MSN, SN, GP, NBGP, ABB, ACC, SNCP, MAE, PVD, AB) are shown as supplied. Their full names were not given, so they are not spelled out.
9. **Guarantees**: the 5-year badge appears where the poster shows it, plus all main-door locks in Categories 1–6 (the supplier says "all our locks"). It is not applied to padlocks, accessories, or the TM53/T189 locks.
10. **Contact**: the phone number (0711 77 66 88) and "We are located along Kijabe Street" come from the posters. There is no website, email or WhatsApp link, because none was supplied.
