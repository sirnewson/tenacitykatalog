Before you choose

Pick the right lock for the door

Which section fits your door?

Bedrooms, offices and interior doors01, 02
Bathrooms and toilets01 (keyless TM53)
Light-duty steel or light-duty solid wooden doors03 — Category 1
Steel doors and heavy-duty wooden doors04–08 — Categories 2 to 6
Highest-spec main door, stainless steel lock case09 — Category 7
Extra locking point on any door10–12 Deadlocks
Wider doors or replacement cylinders15 Cylinders
Gates, stores and shutters16–18 Padlocks

Guarantees & cylinder sizes

  • All standard locks in the main-door range come with a 5-year guarantee.
  • Premium SUS 304 locks (Category 7) carry a lifetime guarantee, subject to supplier terms.
  • Complete lock units include a standard 70 mm cylinder.
  • For doors wider than 2 inches, longer cylinders in 80 mm, 90 mm or 100 mm are sold separately (see section 15).
  • The included 70 mm cylinder cannot be exchanged or traded in for a longer size.
